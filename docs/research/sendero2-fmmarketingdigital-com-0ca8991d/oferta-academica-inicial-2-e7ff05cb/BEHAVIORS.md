# Inicial 2 — behaviors

- Hero and story headings reveal on scroll through the existing blur/letter motion ports.
- The image rail advances with a delayed, interruptible transform and pauses while the user hovers or focuses it.
- Requirements open and close with a mounted height/opacity transition and expose `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, and `aria-hidden` state.
- Arrow buttons use the existing hover motion and all navigations remain ordinary internal links.
